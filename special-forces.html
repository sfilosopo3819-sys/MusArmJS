Special Forces

Master your potential.

The pinnacle of individual instruction at The Music Army. Tailored for advanced players seeking mastery, this tier focuses on nuanced expression, complex genre-specific techniques, intricate soloing, professional tone shaping, and performance readiness for major showcases.

Advanced musicians, seasoned hobbyists chasing technical mastery, or students prepping for auditions, college programs, and headline recitals.
