Discovery level

Infantry

Discover the joy of making music.

Designed for absolute beginners or anyone testing the waters with a brand-new instrument. Infantry breaks down the intimidation barrier with basic posture, instrument handling, and simplified chunks of favorite songs from day one. Theory stays minimal and is introduced intuitively through the music.

Ideal for

Complete beginners, young children exploring an instrument for the first time, or adults wanting a low-pressure trial run.

Program details

Sessions
6 one-on-one sessions, 1 hour each
Schedule
Twice a week
Validity
Use within 1 month

Objectives

  • Proper instrument ergonomics, grip, and posture
  • Basic rhythm counting and core coordination
  • Play 1–2 simplified sections of favorite songs by ear
