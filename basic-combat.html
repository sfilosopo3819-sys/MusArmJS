Foundation level

Basic Combat

Build your musical foundation.

Moving past initial curiosity, this tier locks in the fundamentals. Students build essential muscle memory, learn clean finger and hand transitions, and dive deeper into basic music mechanics. The focus shifts to playing full song verses and choruses with consistent timing and steady rhythm.

Ideal for

Students who have completed Infantry, or self-taught hobbyists looking to clean up bad habits and build a solid technical base.

Program details

Sessions
8 one-on-one sessions, 1 hour each
Schedule
Twice a week
Validity
Use within 1 month

Objectives

  • Clean chord transitions, scale patterns, or foundational drumming rudiments
  • Develop an internal metronome and rhythm consistency
  • Read basic chord charts, tablature, or fundamental sheet music
