A-Team

Accelerate your musical growth.

The bridge from casual hobbyist to confident player. A-Team introduces dynamic control, finger dexterity, and song interpretation. Students tackle more intricate arrangements, explore the basics of improvisation, and start building performance stamina for real-world playing.

Ideal for

Dedicated students ready to commit to accelerated progress and intermediate skill milestones.

Program details

Sessions
15 one-on-one sessions, 1 hour each
Schedule
Twice a week
Validity
Use within 1 month

Objectives

  • Advanced dexterity: complex strumming patterns, multi-octave scales, syncopated rhythms
  • Play complete, challenging songs with dynamic variance
  • Intro to jamming, ear-tuning, and melodic interpretation
